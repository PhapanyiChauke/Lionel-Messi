# Lionel-Messi
Inter Miami Sports Poster

I designed this because Lionel Messi has announced his retirement from international football with Argentina. His journey in the blue and white has come to it's final chapter.

Lionel Messi has given to millions of football fans around the world.

A legacy that will never be forgotten.

His football journey with Argentina has come to an end, but his achievements, his World Cup triumph and the memories he created will remain part of football history forever.

Yet, he is not retiring from his football career with Inter Miami, where his dream in pink is still alive.
